## 2.2 Competitive Analysis
I researched Kenya-based and diaspora-facing competitors offering similar US-bound study/summer programs:

| Company | Instagram | Approx. Following | Positioning | Gap Identified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crimson Education (Africa) | @crimson.za | ~18K | Premium admissions consultancy, polished editorial content, broad "top university" positioning | Global brand, not Kenya-founded; positioned around elite admissions, not a specific summer-program experience |
| Imperial Education Consultants | @imperialeducationconsultants | ~3.6K | High posting frequency, consultation/sales-led | Smaller reach; content leans informational over emotionally resonant |
| Worldwide Education Consultants (WEC) | @worldwide_education_consultant | ~171 | Long-established (since 2001), credibility-based | Minimal, underused social presence — a visible gap |

**Conclusion drawn:** None of the identified competitors combine (a) a Kenya/diaspora-founded brand, (b) a specific, relatable summer-program story (rather than a broad admissions pitch), and (c) content that speaks directly to parental anxiety and distance — particularly for diaspora parents. This is the gap HSSP's content can occupy.
