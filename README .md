# 🧩 1. Overview
The purpose of this challenge was to design original creative content for Vunoh High School Summer Program (HSSP) and build a 2 month campaign plan around it. To demonstrate both creative and strategic thinking within a 60 hour timeline.

The primary objectives were:
- Understand HSSP's positioning through the provided product documentation
- Research the competitive landscape to identify a genuine angle of differentiation
- Design original creative content. No AI-generated material reflecting that angle
- Build a SMART, budgeted, two-month campaign plan with weekly reporting structure

# 🔍 2. Research Phase

## 2.1 Product Understanding
HSSP Vunoh Global offers  placement and support services for short residential summer program hosted by US Universities that typically runs for 2-6 weeks. Places Kenyan and Diaspora high school students into reputable summer programs and manages the full journey on the family's behalf, from first entry all through to the student's safe arrival. HSSP serves as single accountable service , where rather than handing the family a list of programs and stepping back, HSSP manages and stays accountable end to end. From Application, Consultation, Vetting, Visa and Arrival.
The program combines personalized program matching, comprehensive application support, visa application and travel coordination. This allows parents to focus on preparing their child for an exciting international experience while we manage the complex administrative prossess

## 2.2 Competitive Analysis
I researched Kenya-based and diaspora-facing competitors offering similar US-bound study/summer programs:

| Company | Positioning | Gap Identified |
|---|---|---|
| Crimson Education (Africa) | Premium admissions consultancy, polished editorial content, broad "top university" positioning | Global brand, not Kenya-founded; positioned around elite admissions, not a specific summer-program experience |
| Imperial Education Consultants | High posting frequency, consultation/sales led | Smaller reach; content leans informational over emotionally resonant |
| Worldwide Education Consultants (WEC) | Long-established (since 2001), credibility based | Minimal, underused social presence |

**Conclusion drawn:** None of the identified competitors combine (1) a Kenya/diaspora-founded brand, (2) a specific, relatable summer-program story rather than a broad admissions pitch, (3) content that speaks directly to parental anxiety and distance. Particularly for diaspora parents. This is the gap HSSP's content can occupy.

# 🎯 3. Creative Direction & Rationale

**Chosen angle:** Relief for the parents/family, anchored in Vunoh's end-to-end support across the HSSP journey. Positioned specifically for Kenyan and diaspora parents/family, for whom distance and trust are the primary anxiety.

**Why this angle:** Manages the journey from the first enquiry up until the student safely arrives on campus and it addresses the gap identified in the competitor analysis above

**Format chosen:** Poster. Able to complete within the given time period

**Brand execution:** Vunoh blue (#1B75BB) used as dominant / accent color alongside black and white, per brand guidelines.

# 🔁 6. Key Decisions & Trade-offs

- A poster design was prefered given the time constraints hence able to put all my creativity efforts on a single poster.
- decided to go for the parent/ family emotional angle to show relatability and to capture their attention since it stems from solving a a major problem (dealing with the complex administrative processes.)
